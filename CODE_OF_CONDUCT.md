# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate
- Use welcoming language
- Show empathy and patience
- Accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the community

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment or exclusionary behavior will not be tolerated. This includes:

- Offensive comments related to gender, identity, sexual orientation, disability, race, or religion
- Deliberate misuse of power or privilege
- Personal attacks or abusive language

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening an issue and tagging maintainers, or email the project owner if contact info is available.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit comments, block users, or close issues/PRs as necessary to maintain a safe environment.
